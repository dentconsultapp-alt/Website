Delete Your Account

DentConsult · Last updated: 2 September 2026

This page explains how you can delete your DentConsult account and the personal data associated with it. It applies to the DentConsult mobile app (package name com.dentconsultapp.ai) and the DentConsult platform (the "Service"), operated by DentConsult.

Option 1: Delete your account from within the app

  1. Open the DentConsult app and sign in with your registered mobile number.
  2. Go to the Menu tab.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Read the on-screen information and confirm the deletion.

Once confirmed, your account is deleted and you are signed out.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us. Please write from the email address linked to your account, or include your registered mobile number so we can verify your identity.

  1. Send an email to dentconsultapp@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my DentConsult account".
  2. Include your registered mobile number so we can locate your account.
  3. We will verify your request and delete your account within 30 days, and confirm by email.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove the personal data associated with it, including:

  • Your profile details — name, mobile number, email address, professional role, clinic or consultant details, and qualifications.
  • Photos and documents you uploaded (for example, profile photos and professional certificates).
  • Your job and consultation activity and in-app chat messages.
  • Your approximate and precise location data.

What data may be kept, and for how long

We may retain a limited amount of data where we are required to by law or for legitimate business needs:

  • Transaction and payment records — records of consultations, fees, and payments may be retained for up to the period required by applicable tax, accounting, and legal obligations (typically up to 8 years). Sensitive payment credentials are held by our payment partner (Razorpay), not by DentConsult.
  • Records needed to resolve disputes or prevent fraud and abuse — retained only for as long as necessary for those purposes.

Data retained for these purposes is kept secure and is deleted once it is no longer required.

Deleting your DentConsult account does not automatically cancel any active payment mandate (such as a UPI AutoPay subscription) set up with your bank or payment provider. If you have one, please cancel it separately through your bank or UPI app.
