Pricing

DentConsult · Last updated: 24 June 2026

DentConsult connects dental clinics with dental consultants. The consultation fee for each request is set within the platform. DentConsult charges a small platform fee on each completed transaction.

How Pricing Works

ItemAmount
Consultation fee paid by the ClinicAs shown for each consultation request
DentConsult platform fee1% of the consultation amount
Amount paid out to the ConsultantConsultation amount minus the platform fee (and applicable payment-gateway charges)

Example

If a Clinic pays ₹100 for a consultation, DentConsult retains a ₹1 platform fee (1%), and the Consultant receives the remaining amount. The platform fee is deducted from the Consultant's share, not added on top of what the Clinic pays.

Payments

All payments are processed securely through our payment partner, Razorpay. There are no hidden charges beyond the platform fee and any standard payment-gateway charges that may apply.

Taxes

Applicable taxes, if any, will be charged in accordance with Indian law.
