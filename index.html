Built on real research

We asked 1,000 dentists what they actually need

Before building DentConsult, we ran a nationwide email survey of 1,000 dentists across India — clinic owners and visiting specialists alike — to find where the real friction is in everyday practice. Every core feature here comes straight from what they told us.

  • “Scheduling patients I can’t treat myself costs me.” On-demand specialist matching, with an SOS lane for emergencies
  • “Finding and trusting a consultant takes too long.” Profiles, ratings & reliability scores — apply and accept in one tap
  • “Getting paid is slow and unclear.” Instant, transparent payments — 99% to the consultant, a flat 1% fee
What is DentConsult

Like ride-hailing — but for dental specialists

A clinic needs an extra expert for a tricky root canal, a surgery, or a busy day. Nearby specialists see the request, apply, and the clinic picks one. Simple, fast, and built for real clinical life.

For Clinics

Run a clinic and need an extra pair of expert hands? Post a job and let qualified specialists come to you.

  • Post a regular job or an instant SOS emergency request
  • Compare applicants by distance, price, rating & reliability
  • Accept with one tap — keep a backup pool open
  • Pay the agreed amount securely after the work is done

For Consultants

An MDS/BDS specialist who visits multiple clinics? Find paid work nearby that fits your expertise.

  • Get notified the moment a matching job is posted
  • Showcase your profile, cases & star rating
  • Apply in one tap, manage your calendar & conflicts
  • Get paid straight to your bank — 99% of every job
Everything built in

Powerful features, beautifully simple

From secure sign-in to live notifications, DentConsult handles the busywork so you can focus on patients.

One-tap secure login

Sign in by mobile OTP, then unlock instantly with Face ID or fingerprint on return visits.

Built-in messaging

Chat directly with the clinic or consultant, share photos and X-rays, and keep everything in one thread.

Push notifications

Get alerted the instant a job, application, acceptance, or payment happens — even with the app closed.

Live maps & distance

See every clinic and consultant on a map with the real distance in km, powered by OpenStreetMap.

Calendar & history

Day, week, month or agenda views of your bookings, plus a tidy history of every completed job.

Reliability ratings

Star reviews and a transparent cancellation rate let both sides judge reliability before committing.

How it works

From need to done in four steps

1

Post a job

The clinic describes the work, speciality needed, date and budget — or fires off an SOS.

2

Get matched

Nearby specialists are notified instantly and apply. The clinic reviews and accepts the best fit.

3

Do the work

Both sides see it on their calendar and chat to coordinate. The consultant visits and treats.

4

Pay & rate

The clinic pays the agreed amount securely, the consultant gets paid, and both leave a rating.

⚡ The SOS fast-lane

A patient needs urgent specialist help and you can't wait. Fire an SOS and nearby experts are alerted immediately — designed to get help to your chair in under an hour.
